A trust layer for AI in lifestyle medicine.

The model isn't the moat. The relationships are. Palonur is a verification pipeline that puts a named Stanford faculty member behind every answer an AI gives about your sleep, and refuses cleanly when no faculty-grounded answer exists. The defensibility is the rolodex of researchers who agreed to sign.

Tech stack — the verification pipeline

Curated corpusFaculty-selected papers, talks, and clinical guidance, embedded into a vector store keyed by pillar (sleep first, six more queued).Off-the-shelf models can't search this — it didn't exist before us.
Structured outputLLM forced to emit five labeled sections (ANSWER · CITATION · PAPER · FINDING · INTERPRETATION). Two refusal modes (REFUSE, UNCOVERED).Lets us audit every answer and lets partners parse them deterministically.
Faculty-in-the-loopEvery interpretation passes through the named faculty's queue before it ships. Steward console, edit history, approval state.A model can't fake this — it's a workflow, not a prompt.
Refusal postureIf the answer isn't in the faculty corpus, we say so. No silent confabulation, no generic medical hand-wave.This is what makes regulators, insurers, and clinicians take the meeting.
Receipts APIEvery response carries a graph receipt: question → corpus chunk → paper → faculty signer → timestamp. Queryable by partners.The receipt is the product. The chat is the demo.
Two endpoints/api/sleep-agent (consumer-facing answers) · /api/agent/spec (machine-readable for AI platforms, wearables, insurer bots).B2C and B2B share one source of truth — same answer, same signer, same receipt.

The moat — concentric circles of trust, not a feature list

1
Stanford Lifestyle Medicine, today. Six named faculty have signed onto Palonur — Fredericson, Friedlander, Zeitzer, Kluger, Dehghani, and Parker (Advisor). Sleep ships with Prof. Jamie Zeitzer's name on every answer.
2
Stanford, beyond Lifestyle Medicine. Six more pillars (exercise, nutrition, stress, social, substances, environment) — each gets its own Stanford faculty lead. Recruiting is warm-intro through the existing six.
3
Beyond Stanford — global tier-1 faculty. Once a Stanford lead is in place per pillar, we add international peer institutions (Oxford, Karolinska, UCSF, Johns Hopkins, Mayo). Each adds geographic credibility and answer breadth.
4
The receipts compound. Every signed answer is a piece of public-record IP that competitors cannot synthesize after the fact. The corpus, the signers, and the audit trail accrete monthly.

A new entrant can copy the model. They cannot copy "Prof. Jamie Zeitzer signed this answer." That signature is a relationship, and the relationship took us a year of in-person work at Stanford to earn.
