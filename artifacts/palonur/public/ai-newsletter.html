Palonur For SLM leadership · Confidential
A proposal for Stanford Lifestyle Medicine

An AI-augmented newsletter — written by your experts, produced by Palonur.

Thank you for considering how Palonur might help Stanford Lifestyle Medicine publish its newsletter. To be clear from the outset, this is a model of augmentation, not automation. The articles remain the work of SLM's pillar owners and faculty experts, and a human editor reviews and approves every piece before it reaches a reader. Palonur is the engine underneath — it removes the production friction that makes a high-quality newsletter so hard to sustain, so a small team can publish a compelling, credible issue on a dependable cadence.

The case for it is practical. Because faculty already manage their papers and articles inside Palonur, contributing to the newsletter becomes an extension of work they are doing anyway rather than a new obligation. Palonur turns rough notes — or an existing paper — into a clean, consistently formatted draft for the editor to refine, compressing hours of layout and copy-editing into minutes. In parallel, it scans the web for new articles and mentions by SLM members and offers them to editors as ready-to-use sources, so no one loses an afternoon hunting for material. The result is lower production effort, more consistent quality and cadence, and a newsletter that is genuinely feasible with the team and budget you have today.

How it would work — three places Palonur augments

The clearest way to describe the model is by who it helps. Palonur augments three groups — and replaces none of them.

The faculty and experts who write it. Their papers and articles already live in Palonur, so contributing is an extension of work they do anyway, not a new obligation. An expert drafts an article — or drops in a few rough notes — and Palonur returns a clean, formatted draft in minutes. It can rewrite dense passages in plainer language, translate a piece into Spanish, French, or German, and check sources and facts before it is handed in. In parallel, Palonur scans the web for new work by SLM members and offers it to editors, so no contribution goes unnoticed. The expertise — and the byline — stay entirely theirs.

The editor who runs it. Every article and submission arrives in a single review queue. Palonur reads each one, tags who it is for, and assembles a tailored edition for each audience — men, women, athletes, clinical experts, and more — that the editor approves before anything sends. The layout, copy-editing, fact-checking, and formatting that once took days happen in the background, so one editor can sustain a polished, on-brand issue on a dependable cadence — without losing control of a single word.

The business behind it. Palonur runs the subscription and payment infrastructure intelligently — sign-ups, plans, secure checkout, renewals, and receipts handled end to end. Its AI keeps revenue healthy by recovering failed payments, flagging subscribers at risk of leaving, and suggesting the right plan and price for each audience. The newsletter not only reaches more people; it can begin to support itself — every action reviewable by the team.

Taken together, these augmentations let the team you have today publish a compelling, credible newsletter: operations are streamlined, quality and consistency rise, and the whole effort becomes feasible with limited resources. Every step is AI-assisted but human-approved — experts write, the editor decides, and nothing reaches a reader without a person's sign-off.
